What this report does not establish

  • This is an example of the deliverable, issued on cascade's own synthetic held-out corpus (120 records, 5 fields) and signed with the repository's key: the figures illustrate what a client receives, they are not a client result. A client's report is issued on the client's records, and only a report naming the client stands for anything.
  • Measured on 120 record(s) supplied by the client (corpus-heldout.csv, sha256 544d99d9bf24d304…) on 2026-09-03; nothing is claimed about records outside that set.
  • The non-inferiority margin of 2 point(s) is the client's declaration, not a measurement.
  • 0 of 13 assumptions were supplied by the client; the others are repository defaults, and every currency figure moves with them.
  • Latencies are medians measured one item at a time on the measuring machine; queueing under load is a different measurement and is not in this report.
  • rules competes on birth, document only — not measured on name, country, address.
  • Aiming at the record and maximising the field average give routings this sample cannot tell apart (3 gained, 0 lost, p = 0.250); the recommended routing is the one that minimises processing plus exposure, and the record-aimed one is reported beside it when it differs.
  • No current chain was supplied (no --sorties file): the saving has no baseline, and no payback is quoted.
  • The abstention rule counts string signals on the delivered value; it withholds what looks doubtful, which is not the same set as what is wrong — the curve shows both.
